Dual-ESP Flipper Expansion — Wiring Diagram

Flipper Zero powering ESP32-C5 DevKit (justcallmekoko Marauder) and ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N16R8 bare module (Ghost ESP Revival). Every one of the 19 wires (W1 through W18, plus W1b) is individually drawn and labeled. No external power, no switch. C5 is commanded over Flipper UART pins 13 and 14. S3 is commanded over its own GhostNet WiFi AP.

Parts & components to gather

Tick each box as you find it. Every item here should already be on your bench — nothing new to buy.

Modules

Capacitors

  • 10 µF electrolytic × 1C2 — C5 local 3.3 V bulk

Resistors

  • 10 kΩ 1/4 W resistor × 2R1 (S3 EN pull-up) and R2 (S3 IO0 pull-up)

Tools for verification

  • Multimeter × 1continuity + DC voltage + ohms modes — used for every step of the pre-power-on checklist

Pre-power-on checklist (do before enabling Flipper 5 V)

  1. Visually count all 19 wires against the table above — any wire not on the list → remove.
  2. Electrolytic polarity: stripe/short leg of C1, C2, C3 points at GND.
  3. D1 Schottky orientation: banded end (cathode) faces the 5 V rail; unbanded end (anode) faces Flipper Pin 1.
  4. Verify AMS1117 pinout with DMM continuity — never trust silkscreen alone.
  5. Inspect AMS1117 output-cap type: if only MLCC ceramic on the output, physically place C3 directly across AMS1117 OUT → GND pins to prevent LDO oscillation.
  6. Short check (power off): DMM continuity — 5V rail ↔ GND rail no short; 3V3 rail ↔ GND rail no short.
  7. S3 EN pin: DMM in ohms — EN → 3V3 rail ≈ 10 kΩ. EN → GND starts low (C7 charging) then climbs past 1 MΩ within seconds — that's normal.
  8. S3 IO0 pin: DMM in ohms — IO0 → 3V3 rail ≈ 10 kΩ. IO0 → GND ≈ open.
  9. Flipper 5 V output OFF: it's disabled by default — keep it off until all checks pass.
  10. Confirm the old GPIO0→GND flashing jumper has been removed from the S3.
  11. Flipper battery ≥ 80% charge (low battery + radio TX = Flipper brownout risk).
Operational rule: do not fire Marauder deauth/beacon flood simultaneously with Ghost ESP BLE spam. Worst-case dual-radio TX (~900 mA–1.1 A) exceeds Flipper's 5 V budget (~800 mA peak). Sequential use, or one TXing while the other scans, is fine.

Wardriving add-on — GPS + microSD breadboard wiring

Adds a NEO-M8N GPS (UART1 on S3 IO4/IO5) and a microSD module (SPI2 on S3 IO10–IO13) for wardriving — PCAP capture + WiGLE CSV export through Ghost ESP Revival. Both modules attach to the S3 only; the C5/Marauder side is untouched. GPS pulls from the existing 3.3 V rail (AMS1117 OUT); SD pulls from the 5 V rail (post-Schottky) so write spikes don't yank the S3's 3.3 V supply.

Protoboard layout (soldered) — top-down view

Same electrical net as the breadboard above, but laid out for a soldered perfboard. Modules sit in fixed positions; jumper wires (under the board) route between solder pads. Decoupling caps sit on the top side directly next to the module they protect — never on a rail far away. The patch antenna on the NEO-M8N is held off the C5/S3 PCBs by ≥30 mm to keep digital noise off the L1 GPS band.

Trigger this stage only after the breadboard prototype passes — do not solder until Stage 1 (GPS fix in Web UI) and Stage 2 (SD mounted, valid PCAP/CSV) are both verified on the breadboard. Once soldered, repeat the pre-power-on checklist before re-enabling Flipper 5 V.
