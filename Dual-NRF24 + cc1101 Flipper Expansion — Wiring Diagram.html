Dual-NRF24 Flipper Expansion — Wiring Diagram

Flipper Zero (Momentum) driving CC1101 (Sub-GHz) + NRF24 #1 PA+LNA (2.4 GHz) + TSOP38238 (IR RX). A separate ESP32-S3 Super Mini powered from the same Flipper 5 V rail hosts a second NRF24 #2 PA+LNA on its own SPI bus, driven by the ported W0rthlessS0ul/nRF24_jammer firmware. Two independent radios, two independent LDOs, zero shared-bus contention. All passives below are from the existing BOM — nothing new to buy.

Operational rule: at most ONE of the three 2.4 GHz radios (NRF24 #1 TX, NRF24 #2 TX, S3 WiFi TX burst) may transmit at a time. All three at full TX exceeds the Flipper pin-1 5 V budget (~500 mA sustained). Sniff + jam + WiFi-AP-idle is safe. Three-way TX will brown the Flipper.
Post-review changes (applied after hardware-wiring-reviewer pass):
  • UART wires W23/W24 removed — S3 GPIO3 is a strap pin; Flipper TX driving it at boot is a blocker. Control the S3 via its WiFi AP only.
  • R1, R2 added — 10 kΩ pull-ups on CC1101 CSN (Pin 4) and NRF24 #1 CSN (Pin 7) to AMS 3.3 V rail (uses your two existing 10 kΩ resistors). Prevents floating-CSN latch-up at power-on.
  • C9 added — additional 10 µF electrolytic at NRF24 #2 VCC (paired with C8). Brings NRF24 #2 decoupling up to Nordic's recommended ≥10 µF + 1 µF spec. Resolves the original BOM gap. (C2 stays on the 5 V rail — original placement was correct.)
  • Rail voltage annotation corrected: post-Schottky ≈ 4.55 V under load (not 4.7 V — 1N5819 Vf is 0.35–0.45 V at 200–300 mA). AMS1117 dropout margin ≈ 150 mV — thin but functional.
Remaining residual risk (not BOM-fixable):
  • No hardware interlock against three-way TX overload. The "one radio TX at a time" rule is enforced only by operator discipline (don't fire Flipper NRF24 TX while the S3 jam is running and S3 has an active WiFi client). A polyfuse or S3-gated load switch on NRF24 #2 VCC would harden this. Monitor for Flipper resets during dual-radio sessions; if it happens, you've hit the limit.
  • AMS1117 margin is tight. 4.55 V − 1.1 V dropout = 3.45 V min; expected output 3.25–3.35 V. Ribbon sag under TX burst could briefly push AMS into dropout. Keep the ribbon short and re-measure under NRF24 #1 TX during bring-up.

Pre-power-on checklist (do BEFORE enabling Flipper 5 V)

  1. Count all wires against the W1–W31 table above. Any extra wire → remove and re-check.
  2. Verify D1 orientation: banded end (cathode) faces the 5 V rail; unbanded end (anode) faces Flipper Pin 1.
  3. Electrolytic polarity: stripe/short leg of C1, C3, C5 points at GND. A reversed electrolytic can vent.
  4. DMM continuity check (power off): 5 V rail ↔ GND rail = open. AMS 3.3 V rail ↔ GND rail = open. S3 3V3 pin ↔ GND = open.
  5. DMM continuity: NRF24 #1 CSN (pin 7) NOT shorted to CC1101 CSN (pin 4). Critical — shared-bus integrity depends on CS isolation.
  6. DMM continuity: no NRF24 VCC (either #1 or #2) accidentally on the 5 V rail. NRF24 is 3.3 V STRICT; 5 V will destroy it.
  7. Verify AMS1117 module is the 3.3 V fixed variant (not 5.0 or ADJ).
  8. Flipper Pin 1 5 V is OFF by default — leave it off until all checks pass.
  9. S3 Super Mini: USB-C detached for first power-on (prevents dual-feed contention between USB and Flipper 5 V).
  10. Flipper battery ≥ 80 %. Low battery + 2.4 GHz TX = brownout risk.
  11. Confirm Momentum setting: Settings → Protocols → GPIO Pins → NRF24 SPI → Extra 7. Save.

Bring-up sequence (power-on verification, in order)

  1. Flipper only (S3 not powered yet, Pin 1 5 V on): measure post-Schottky rail = 4.70–4.85 V. AMS 3.3 V rail = 3.25–3.35 V. If anything sags, stop and debug.
  2. Launch Flipper IR app → learn a TV remote → confirms TSOP38238 + pin 15 wiring.
  3. Launch Flipper Sub-GHz → Radio Settings → Module: External → scan 433 MHz → CC1101 works.
  4. Launch NRF24 Sniffer FAP → confirm Momentum detects the NRF24 #1 radio → sniff a known dongle.
  5. Disable Pin 1 5 V. Connect S3 via USB-C only. Confirm jammer AP appears. Browse http://192.168.4.1.
  6. Trigger a short S3 jam burst on an unused channel → verify on any 2.4 GHz scanner.
  7. Disconnect S3 USB. Re-enable Pin 1 5 V. S3 should now boot from Flipper power; AP should re-appear. Post-LDO S3 3V3 pin measures 3.25–3.35 V.
  8. Dual-radio test: Flipper NRF24 Sniffer running on channel X; on S3 web UI, launch jam on channel Y (≠ X). Flipper sniff must continue without SPI corruption, Flipper must not reset, S3 must not brown out.
  9. Soak: run above dual-radio config for 10 minutes. Feel AMS1117 body — warm is OK, untouchable means overloaded.
Firmware note: the S3 side is the ported W0rthlessS0ul/nRF24_jammer. GPIO remap required (SCK=12, MOSI=11, MISO=13, CS=10, CE=9). PlatformIO env: esp32-s3-devkitc-1. Build with python -m platformio run -e esp32-s3-super-mini on Windows (not pio — avoids the self-replace lock).
